# Meridian Freightways - Logistics & Supply Chain Dashboard | Power BI

3-page end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing freight performance for 30 customers and 10 products.

### Business Problem
Meridian was losing money on delays, damages, and cancellations. No visibility on which carriers, regions, and customers were causing it.

### My Solution
Built a 3-page dashboard:
1. **Executive Overview** - KPIs, Revenue vs Shipments, On-Time trend
2. **Carrier & Regional Performance** - Which carrier/region delays most
3. **Customer Performance** - Top 5 customers by Revenue, Shipments, Cancellation, Damage, Delay

### Key Insights
- **Revenue Driver:** Unity Foods Ltd ($7.5K) & Coastal Traders ($7.1K) are top revenue customers
- **Risk:** National Building Merchants 16.67% cancellation rate, AppleGate 11.76% damage rate
- **Operations:** Apex & Ivory Groups 46 delay days each, Avg Delivery 1.56 days
- **Carriers:** Need audit on worst delay carrier from Page 2

### Tools & DAX
- Power BI, Power Query, DAX
- Measures: Total Revenue, Cancellation %, Damage %, Delay Days
